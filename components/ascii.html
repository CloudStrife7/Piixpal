Components/ASCII & dither

ASCII & dither

Moving pictures drawn in type, dots and dither: backgrounds for your sections, and little films with feeling. Everything here plays in four looks (ascii, dither, halftone, pixel), in full colour or one ink.

<piix-backdrop>

Animated ASCII, dither, halftone and pixel backgrounds for your sections.

Your section,
on top.

Options
kind
aurora | sunburst | plasma | rain | stars | clouds | tunnel | synthwave | fire | waves (default aurora)
look
ascii | dither | halftone | pixel (default: the kind's own)
palette
lime, aurora, sunset, ocean, candy, fire, gameboy, paper, mono, or your own colours dark to light: "#000,#f0f,#fff"
ink
draw it all in one colour
paper
the background colour
cell
size of a character, dot or block in px
ramp
ascii characters: classic, soft, blocks, binary, dots, hearts, code, or your own
edges
"off", or a number: how sharp a change becomes a / | \ line
speed
1 is normal, 0 holds still
height
px when not filling (default 320)
fill
sit behind the content of its parent
